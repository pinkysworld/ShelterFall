Remove Ads — buying and restoring

Shelter Fall has exactly one in-app purchase: Remove Ads. It is a one-time, non-consumable purchase tied to your Apple Account — buy it once and it applies to every device signed in with that account, forever. There is no subscription and nothing recurring.

What it does

  • Removes every banner, interstitial and app-open advertisement.
  • Gives you the second-chance revive and the supply drop directly, with no video to watch — the same allowance per run that a free player earns by watching ads.
  • Changes nothing else. Difficulty, enemies, loot and events are identical for everyone.

How to buy it

Open Settings in the game

Tap Settings on the main menu, or the gear in the quick-action bar during a run.

Find the Remove Ads section

The price is shown on the right of the button in your local currency. It varies by country, so it will not always match what you see quoted elsewhere.

Confirm with Apple

Tap the button and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your Apple Account password. Apple handles the payment — the game never sees your payment details.

Ads stop immediately

No restart needed. The banner disappears and the Settings row changes to show the purchase is active.

How to restore it on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple Account

Restoring only works for the account that originally paid. A different account cannot recover the purchase.

Open Settings → Restore Purchases

Tap it once and wait a few seconds while Apple verifies your purchase history.

Check the result

On success the ads stop and the Remove Ads row shows as owned. Restoring is free and you can do it as often as you like.

Restoring is never a second charge. Apple blocks a repeat purchase of a non-consumable. If you tap Remove Ads again on a device that already owns it, Apple restores it instead of billing you.

If something goes wrong

The purchase went through but ads are still showing

Tap Settings → Restore Purchases first — that resolves it in almost every case.

If it does not, check that the Apple Account under iOS Settings → your name → Media & Purchases is the account you paid with. Then force-close Shelter Fall and reopen it.

Still showing? Email us with the date of purchase and we will help you chase it.

The Remove Ads button is greyed out or shows no price

This means the App Store could not load the product, usually a network problem. Check your connection and reopen the Settings screen — the game retries automatically.

It can also happen if purchases are blocked under iOS Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases.

The price is different from what I saw somewhere else

That is expected. Remove Ads is priced per country, adjusted for local purchasing power rather than a straight currency conversion, so the same product costs noticeably less in some storefronts than in others.

The price shown inside the game, in your own currency, is always the price you pay.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we cannot issue them. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

If the reason is something broken in the game, please email us too. We would rather fix it.

Does it work with Family Sharing?

Remove Ads is not enabled for Family Sharing. Each Apple Account needs its own purchase.

Saves and progress

Does my progress sync between devices?

Yes. Saves sync through iCloud, so a run started on your iPhone can be continued on your iPad when both are signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled.

Sync happens in the background. If a device has been offline, give it a moment on Wi-Fi before expecting the newest save to appear.

I lost my run. Can I get it back?

The game keeps one auto-save, written at the start of each day. Resuming puts you back at the beginning of the day you were on — so the day is replayed, never skipped.

If you deleted the app, the local save is gone with it, but the iCloud copy is restored on reinstall when iCloud Drive is on. Meta progress — points, achievements, unlocks — is separate from a run and survives.

Can I quit during a night and come back?

Yes. Leaving mid-night no longer auto-resolves the battle instantly. When you return you resume at the start of that day, with the day's decisions to make again.

Playing the game

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Shelter Fall plays fully offline. A connection is only used to load ads, sync iCloud saves and process a purchase.

Which difficulty should I start on?

Normal is the intended experience with five survivors. Easy gives you six and a generous stock if you would rather learn the loop first.

Every difficulty runs the full twenty days — harder settings mean thinner margins and bigger waves, not a shorter game.

My shelter keeps falling around day 5. What am I doing wrong?

Almost always defenses. Auto-assign spreads people across gathering jobs and will not build a perimeter for you — you have to put someone on Build Defenses and someone on Repair after a rough night.

Breaches damage the shelter directly once your defenses are gone, and a destroyed shelter ends the run even with every survivor alive. The guide goes through this in detail.

The game crashes or freezes

Force-close and reopen first, then restart the device. Make sure both iOS and Shelter Fall are up to date.

If it persists, email us with your device model, iOS version, and what was on screen. Crash reports help enormously if you have iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers turned on.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17.0 or later.
